# Deliverable restyle brief (guide video, article, click-through demo)

## Why
Reflow Studio's pipeline turns one verified run into four deliverables. Three of them are drawn by this repo and appear large in the product's intro film: the narrated guide video (`render/`), the step-by-step article and the click-through demo (`src/docs.mjs`, `src/py/stills.py`). The founder hates pill/badge styling ("특히 이런 버튼 나 진짜 개싫어해" about rounded status chips with a pastel fill) and anything that reads as AI-generated UI. Current offenders: the guide video's top-left step indicator (dark pill with a cobalt number disc), the article's `N단계` pill badges, full rounded-rectangle highlight boxes with number dots. Restyle them into a calmer, typographic, premium language that matches the intro film. The fourth deliverable (the on-page Reflow player) is the company's real product and is **out of scope** — do not touch `src/reflow.mjs` or anything under `/home/jay/Business/Dev/Reflow-CTO`.

## Visual language (shared with the film)
- Brand cobalt `#2563EB`, deep cobalt `#172554`, ink `#111827`, muted `#5F6B7A`, hairline `#D8DEE8`. No green, no orange, no pastel fills.
- **No pills, no badges, no number discs.** Step numbers are typography: tabular numerals (`01`, `02` …) in cobalt next to the step title, or a quiet `2 / 7`.
- Targets are marked with **corner marks**: four short L-shaped cobalt corners (≈3 px at 1080p, arms ≈16–18 px, small radius at the bend, a soft cobalt glow) around the element, plus a very faint cobalt wash inside. No full outline box.
- Pretendard (installed at `/usr/share/fonts/opentype/pretendard/`).
